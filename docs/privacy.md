# Privacy

*Last updated: August 2026*

This is a personal, non-commercial site published by **Ketan Kamble**. It's built to respect your privacy by default — no tracking cookies, no advertising, no cross-site tracking, nothing sold.

## Analytics

I use **GoatCounter**, a cookieless, privacy-first analytics service, to see aggregate visit numbers — which posts are read, roughly which country visitors come from, and referring sites. Nothing is stored on or read from your device, so there's no consent banner. No personal profiles are built, and you're not tracked across other sites. Your IP is used only for a moment to derive the country, then discarded — the country is kept only as an anonymous count. If your browser sends "Do Not Track," your visit isn't counted at all.

## Cookies & hosting

The site sets no tracking or advertising cookies. Your browser may keep a local preference (like your light/dark theme choice) on your own device — it's never sent anywhere. The site is hosted on **GitHub Pages**, which, like any web host, may process standard technical data (such as IP addresses) in its server logs under its own privacy terms.
