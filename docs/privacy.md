# Privacy & Cookie Policy

*Last updated: July 2026*

This is a personal website published by **Ketan Kamble** at **ketankamble.com**. It's built to respect your privacy by default. This page explains, in plain terms, what data is (and isn't) collected.

## The short version

No tracking cookies. No advertising. No cross-site tracking. No selling of data. I use **privacy-first, cookieless analytics** to see aggregate visit numbers — nothing that identifies you personally.

## Analytics

To understand how the site is used (which posts are read, roughly where visitors come from), I use **cookieless, privacy-first web analytics**. This means:

- **No cookies are set** for analytics, and nothing is stored on your device.
- **No personal profiles** are built, and you are **not** tracked across other websites.
- Only **aggregate, non-identifying** measurements are collected — such as page views, referring sites, and country-level location derived without storing your IP address.

Because this analytics does not use cookies or process personal data in an identifying way, there is no cookie-consent banner to click through.

## Cookies

This site sets **no tracking or advertising cookies**. Your browser may store a small local preference (for example, your light/dark theme choice) on your own device — this stays in your browser, is not sent anywhere, and is not used to identify you.

## Hosting & server logs

The site is hosted on **GitHub Pages** (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web host, GitHub may process standard technical information (such as IP addresses) in its server logs to deliver the site securely and prevent abuse. That processing is governed by GitHub's own privacy practices.

## Forms & personal data

The site does **not** collect personal data through forms — there is no sign-up, no account, and no newsletter form that stores your details here. If a newsletter is offered in future, it will run on a separate platform (e.g. LinkedIn) with its own privacy terms.

## External links

Pages may link to third-party sites such as **LinkedIn** and **GitHub**. Once you follow those links, their own privacy policies apply.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, data-protection laws may give you rights over any personal data relating to you — typically access, correction, erasure, and objection. Because this site is designed not to collect identifying personal data, there is little to act on. If you have any question or request about your data, just reach out (see below) and I'll help.

## Contact

Questions about privacy? The fastest way to reach me is via **[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ketan-kamble-012a1582)**.
